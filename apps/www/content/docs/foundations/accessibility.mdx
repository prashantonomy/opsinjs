The target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA. The authors of the sixty components audited every one against it, in a source pass and a rendered pass, and fixed what they found. That is not an independent review, and no clinician has reviewed any component. opsinjs makes no conformance claim, and nothing here is for a production health surface.

## What components guarantee

- **Status is never colour alone.** A visible word and an icon whose shape differs per level sit beside every status colour.
- **Targets are at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels** at every density.
- **Keyboard patterns come from Base UI,** and only a modal surface traps focus.
- **Motion and translucency degrade per token** when the reader asks for less.
- **A number must never be clipped.** The value and its unit wrap instead.

## What your product owns

- **Names and words.** Label every icon-only button and field, and write headings, link text and a text equivalent for every chart. See [Writing](./writing.mdx).
- **Order and focus.** Tab and screen readers follow the DOM. Place focus after a delete or a submit, and add a skip link and landmarks.
- **Clinical meaning.** opsinjs renders "Needs attention". Which reading earns it is your product's clinical decision.
- **Timing, sessions and sign-in**, under SC 2.2.1 and SC 3.3.8.
- **Anything you colour, animate or make translucent yourself.**

## Contrast

`pnpm run contrast` measures every published token pair in both themes as a WCAG 2.2 ratio and an APCA Lc. Failing pairs are shown, not filtered, and `contrast:verify` fails the nightly job when a token edit moves a figure. The floor is on [Colour](./colour.mdx), and every figure is in the [contrast reference](../reference/generated/contrast.mdx).

Check any pair you invent at [/playground/contrast](/playground/contrast), and text over a translucent rung, whose figure assumes its opaque fallback.

## Colour independence

The words are Steady, Watch, Needs attention, Urgent and Not known, and the `data-status` ids are `steady`, `watch`, `attention`, `urgent` and `unknown`. Under deuteranopia `steady` and `attention` collapse to one tone, so the word and icon carry the difference. Check in greyscale and at [/playground/status](/playground/status). Category colour never shares an element with status. See [Two colour axes](../health/two-colour-axes.mdx).

## Keyboard and focus

A composite widget such as a menu is one <Kbd>Tab</Kbd> stop, and arrow keys, <Kbd>Home</Kbd> and <Kbd>End</Kbd> move inside it. <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> dismisses the topmost surface and returns focus to its trigger. Positive `tabindex` is banned. The focus ring sits outside the element, so keep it clear of `overflow: hidden` and sticky bottom bars (SC 2.4.11).

## Screen readers

A value change is announced politely and a safety change assertively, with no third level. [AlertBanner](../components/alert-banner.mdx) uses `aria-live="polite"` at `attention` and `role="alert"` at `urgent`. Announce a sentence ("Blood pressure, 138 over 84, above your usual range"), never a bare number, and put the status word in the accessible name.

## Target size

`--opsin-target-minimum` is `2.75rem`, 44 pixels at the default root size, so it grows with the reader's text. That is the AAA figure (SC 2.5.5). AA asks for 24 (SC 2.5.8). `--opsin-target-separation` (`0.5rem`) spaces targets drawn smaller than 44 pixels. Never put delete beside save. Pair [Slider](../components/slider.mdx) with [ReadingInput](../components/reading-input.mdx), and give every drag a single-pointer alternative.

## Text resizing and zoom

Text scales to 200% through the root font size, and layouts reflow at 400% zoom in a 1280 pixel window (SC 1.4.10). Fixed pixel heights, `overflow: hidden` and `user-scalable=no` usually break them.

## User preferences

- **`prefers-reduced-motion`.** Durations collapse to `1ms` and springs to their declared fallbacks. The state change still happens.
- **`prefers-reduced-transparency`.** Translucent rungs turn opaque, and nothing moves.
- **`prefers-contrast: more`.** Only [Surface](../components/surface.mdx) and [DisclaimerNote](../components/disclaimer-note.mdx) respond. The token layer has no answer.
- **`forced-colors: active`.** Surface, [Field](../components/field.mdx), [RangeBar](../components/range-bar.mdx), [ScoreDial](../components/score-dial.mdx) and others answer it in their own files.

Animation outside the tokens must read `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)')` itself, and urgency is never carried by motion. See [Motion](./motion.mdx).

## Cognitive accessibility

Ask for one decision per screen, and name the primary action's outcome instead of "Continue". Put the answer before the explanation, and keep the reader's input through an error. [Toast](../components/toast.mdx) dismisses itself after five seconds by default, so anything the reader must act on belongs in an AlertBanner. This guidance is our opinion, untested by user research.

## Print

The product stylesheet prints both themes as ink on white, Surface keeps its outline, and [RelativeTime](../components/relative-time.mdx) prints an absolute time. A printed value still needs its unit, when it was taken, its status word and any disclaimer. Paper cannot be updated, so a provisional value must say so on the printout.

## Testing your integration

opsinjs has no test suite of its own. `pnpm run check:a11y` reads component source on every pull request, and a nightly browser pass checks hit area and 200% text. Assert on roles, accessible names, `data-*` attributes and visible text, which semver covers, and never on class names or DOM nesting.

Test with an automated engine, the keyboard alone, 200% text, 400% zoom, greyscale, each preference and a screen reader, iOS VoiceOver and Android TalkBack first. Use empty, stale, out-of-range and implausible data. Finally, read the screen aloud to an outsider and ask what they would do next.

## Conformance documents

opsinjs makes no conformance claim at any level and publishes no conformance report. An Accessibility Conformance Report would list every one of the 55 WCAG 2.2 Level A and AA criteria as Not Evaluated, because that column records an independent evaluation and none has happened. Your product needs its own statement and report.

## Known gaps

- No screen reader has been used on any component, and no disabled people have tested one.
- Forced colours are untested on a real Windows contrast theme, and print on a real printer.
- Text spacing (SC 1.4.12) is unaddressed.
- No shared announcement helper ships.
