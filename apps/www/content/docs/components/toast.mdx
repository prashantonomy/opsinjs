## What this name refers to

A brief message that appears and disappears on its own.

## Why it is not on the roster

A message that removes itself is the wrong shape for anything about someone's health: the reader who most needs it is the one most likely to miss it. Kept on the roster because products use toasts for save confirmations, which is legitimate.

## What to use instead

Use [AlertBanner](alert-banner.mdx) or [Callout](callout.mdx) instead.
