## Clinical meaning

**Asserts.** That the product has assigned one of exactly four levels of attention to
the thing the pill labels: `steady`, `watch`, `attention` or `urgent`, defined in
[Two colour axes](../health/two-colour-axes.mdx). It does not say what is wrong, why,
or what happens next.

**Never read as.** A diagnosis, a severity score or a triage category. The levels
describe what the interface asks of the reader, not the state of their body, so
`urgent` on a screen is not `urgent` in a clinical handover.

**Colour axis.** Status only, and never alone: every pill renders a word and a glyph
beside its colour. A category colour belongs on a [Badge](./badge.mdx), never on a pill,
and [Two colour axes](../health/two-colour-axes.mdx) says why the two never share an element.

**Thresholds.** None. The consuming product assigns the level from rules or a clinician,
and the pill derives nothing.

<SafetyCallout severity="urgent" evidence="opinion">
At most one `urgent` pill may be visible on a screen. If the product's rules produce
two, the screen needs an [AlertBanner](./alert-banner.mdx) for the most serious one.
See [Alerts](../health/alerts.mdx).
</SafetyCallout>

## Accessibility

The icon is `aria-hidden` and sized in `em`, so the word carries the meaning and the
glyph grows with it. At 200% text the pill grows and wraps, and the word is never
truncated or replaced by the icon alone. Light-mode text contrast passes, with no axe,
target-size, focus or 320px reflow failure.

`pnpm run check:a11y` checks on every commit that no file paints a status colour
without reading `CLINICAL_STATUS_META` and a glyph, that every status surface carries
`data-status`, and that status words come from `CLINICAL_STATUS_META[level].word`, never
a literal. The four levels name four distinct glyphs: circle, ringed dot, diamond and
octagon. A11Y017 fails the build when two levels share one.

**Residual items.**

- The consuming product must pass `describes` when the pill floats free of its subject,
  in a table cell reached by column navigation or a card corner, because the component
  cannot know the reading context.
- With `describes`, the pill announces as *Needs attention, Second example measurement*.
  Omit it when the subject is visible text in the same reading unit.
- The pill is a `span`. One made into a filter is a Button and owes a Button's target
  and name.
- No live region is mounted, on purpose. A static read-only label is not a message, so
  a `role="status"` announcement would be wrong.
- Not measured: dark-mode text contrast, forced colours, and the boundary and glyph's
  non-text contrast against the surface. Those pairs arrive through role tokens and live
  in the token layer and the report below, not in this source.

## Props interface

<PropsTable name="StatusPillProps" />

`status` has no default, and a value outside the four, including `unknown`, renders no
pill and warns in development. `className` is merged last and can strip the
fill or the line, so change `size` instead. It cannot recolour the word; a rule on
`[data-slot="status-pill"]` can.
