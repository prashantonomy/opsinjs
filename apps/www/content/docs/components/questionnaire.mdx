## What this name refers to

A multi-question form for a validated instrument such as a symptom score.

## Why it is not on the roster

Validated instruments are licensed, scored and interpreted by their publishers, and a component that renders one invites a product to alter its wording or its scoring. That is a clinical-safety problem no amount of API design fixes.

## What to use instead

Use [Field](field.mdx) or [LogSheet](log-sheet.mdx) instead.
