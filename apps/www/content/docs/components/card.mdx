## Content guidelines

A card holds one idea. If the title needs *and* in it, it is two cards. Titles
are noun phrases in sentence case. Card ships no copy, so every word inside is
the product's, checked against
[Plain-English A to Z](../content/plain-english-a-z.mdx).

<DoDont>
  <DoDont.Do>
    **"Recent readings"** as a card title, with the readings inside it.
  </DoDont.Do>
  <DoDont.Dont>
    **"Recent readings and upcoming appointments"** in one card. The boundary
    then tells the reader nothing.
  </DoDont.Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

**Checked by `pnpm run check:a11y` on every commit.** Every colour is a role
token, no type size is in `px`, and no banned word appears.

**Argued from the source, not measured.**

- A card is not a landmark or a region: no role and no name unless it is a
  link.
- The heading level is the page's; the title slot resets font and margin, so
  the level is outline only.
- A link card is one control, with a rem floor of `--opsin-target-minimum` on
  both axes and its own `focus-visible` ring.
- The edge survives greyscale, `forced-colors` and print, where a real border
  appears.

**Not checked by any gate.** The 44px floor, the footer separation, the
description and focus ring contrast pairs, the `href` exclusion, and reader
stylesheets.

## API reference

<PropsTable name="CardProps" />

`rung` defaults to `card`, not `raised`, and never goes above `raised`: a
translucent rung warns in development and renders. `render` takes the router's
link element itself, not a function, and does nothing without `href`. There is
no `status`, `variant`, `color` or `category`.
