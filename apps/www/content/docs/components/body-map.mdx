## Clinical meaning

**Asserts.** Only that the reader indicated these regions, not what is there, whether
it hurts, how strongly, since when, or what it means. The shipped labels are generic
placeholders, and the consuming product owns the region words and any interpretation.

**Never read as.** A symptom checker, a triage or a diagnosis. A product that treats
a marked region as evidence of a condition has added a judgement the component does
not make, and owns it.

**Colour axis.** Neither. A selection is a muted fill, a hairline and a tick, never
a hue, and a marked region carries no clinical level and names no category. See
[Two colour axes](../health/two-colour-axes.mdx).

**Thresholds.** None. The component runs no rules over a selection and reaches no
conclusion from it, and whether a selection triggers anything is the product's
decision.

## Accessibility

Each region is a real `button`, named by its label, with `aria-pressed` for its state,
inside a group named by `label`. Unselected shows a plus, the selected state survives
greyscale with `aria-pressed` as its carrier, and each marker meets the 44 by 44 floor
on both axes in `rem`. Every colour is a role token, no type size is in `px`, and no
banned word appears. Left and right are the subject's own sides, so the `left-arm`
marker sits on the viewer's right, matching clinical convention.

The audit fixed one defect. The `rem` markers outgrew the `px`-pinned figure box at
200% text and collided, so they could not be tapped separately (1.4.4, 1.4.10). The
box now scales in `rem` in lockstep with the markers, so the spacing ratio holds at
every text size and the default view is unchanged.

**Residual items.**

- The Front and Back captions are `<p>`, not headings, by design, so the component
  hardcodes no heading level into a host document of unknown depth. Each figure still
  carries a named `role="group"`.
- Screen-reader output in VoiceOver, NVDA and TalkBack, the nine-stop tab sequence and
  forced colours are not gated.
- The paired checkbox list stays the primary path for readers who cannot point at a
  target.

## Props interface

<PropsTable name="BodyMapProps" />

`label` is required and has no default. Name it as the question the reader is
answering. A region `key` with no built-in place on the figure is warned once in
development and skipped rather than drawn. `view` defaults to `both`.
