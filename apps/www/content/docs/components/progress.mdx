## What this name refers to

How far through something the reader is.

## Why it is not on the roster

Delegated to Base UI. Never use it to show a health value: a progress bar implies a target, and most readings do not have one.

## What to use instead

Use [RangeBar](range-bar.mdx) or [ScoreDial](score-dial.mdx) instead.
