<ComponentInstall

```tsx
import { ResultCard } from "@/components/ui/result-card"
import { EXAMPLE_SOURCE } from "@/lib/opsinjs"
```

```tsx
<ResultCard
  title="Example measurement"
  value={14}
  unit="mg/dL"
  precision={0}
  measuredAt="2026-03-14T08:12:00+00:00"
  now="2026-03-14T11:12:00+00:00"
  range={{ low: 10, high: 20, source: EXAMPLE_SOURCE }}
  status="steady"
  category="labs"
  meaning="Two or three short sentences, in the second person, written by the product."
/>
```

## Clinical meaning

**Asserts.** A measurement was taken, at a time, in a unit, with any stated range and
the level the product assigned.
[Every number](../health/numbers.mdx) goes through Value.

**Never read as.** A diagnosis, a health summary or a clinician's advice. A next step
is the product's, and your `meaning` and action labels say who is speaking. A result
that may distress needs [High-stakes moments](../health/high-stakes-moments.mdx)
around it.

**Colour axis.** Both, as [Two colour axes](../health/two-colour-axes.mdx) requires:
status reaches only the [StatusPill](./status-pill.mdx), category only the title, and
no prop tints the surface.

**Thresholds.** The product's. No default [range](../health/reference-ranges.mdx),
status derivation ([Two colour axes](../health/two-colour-axes.mdx)) or staleness
boundary ([Trends and uncertainty](../health/trends.mdx)) exists here;
`staleAfterHours` passes through to [RelativeTime](./relative-time.mdx). A reading you
cannot vouch for must not drive `attention` or `urgent`.

**Provenance and privacy.** `provenance` is free text with no
[class](../health/trends.mdx), so keep an estimated or self-reported value free of
`status` yourself. It has no mask; [Consent and privacy](../health/consent-and-privacy.mdx) is
built around the card.

## Accessibility

The audit changed nothing, finding no axe violation, target below the 44px floor or
focus-visible gap. The title is a real heading at the caller's level, the
[StatusPill](./status-pill.mdx) carries a word and an icon, and the card reflows
unclipped at 320px and 200%.

**Residual items.**

- By design, the root `<article>` takes `aria-label` from the title, not
  `aria-labelledby`, matching the visible heading. A visible-heading reference is a
  future enhancement.
- `className` merges onto the root, so a caller's `truncate` can clip a reading. The
  component truncates nothing; the JSDoc warns of it.
- No gate checks print, `forced-colors` or `prefers-contrast`.

## Props interface

<PropsTable name="ResultCardProps" />

`now` and `precision` are required: the card never reads the clock, so pass one
`now` to every card on a screen. `value` is `number | null`, never a string; a
pair is `segments`. A third action, a second `recommended` and a `titleLevel`
outside 2 to 6 each warn once and fall back.
