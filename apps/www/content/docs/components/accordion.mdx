## Accessibility

Axe, target-size, focus-visible and 320px reflow checks were clean. Base UI
renders each header as an `h3` with a `button`, wires `aria-expanded` and
`aria-controls` between trigger and panel, and hides the chevron with
`aria-hidden`, so a reader relies on `aria-expanded`, not the glyph. The
chevron's turn uses `--opsin-duration-fast`, collapsed under
`prefers-reduced-motion`, and a closed panel is unmounted, leaving the tab order.
The audit fixed one SC 2.4.7 defect: the trigger's focus ring was drawn outside
the trigger, where the root's `overflow-hidden` clipped it, and it now renders
inside, whole on every straight edge.

**Residual items.**

- A small notch of the focus ring is still clipped at the first and last sections' outer corners.
- Every contrast pair against the card surface is unmeasured, not known-failing.
- That no must-see content may sit in a closed panel stays a docs contract the wrapper cannot enforce.

## Props interface

<PropsTable name="AccordionProps" />

`multiple` defaults to `false`. `value` without `onValueChange` fixes the open
sections as given. An empty `items` array renders nothing and warns in
development, as does a duplicated `value`.
