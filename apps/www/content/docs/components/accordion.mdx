## Content guidelines

Write headers as a scannable index: a question, a noun phrase or a setting name,
in sentence case, without terminal punctuation. Never fold a warning or a step the
reader must take into a panel that starts closed. It belongs in a
[Callout](./callout.mdx) that stays open.

<DoDont>
  <DoDont.Do>
    **"When can I reach the team?"** The reader's own question, short enough to
    scan against its siblings.
  </DoDont.Do>
  <DoDont.Dont>
    **A "More" panel holding "Call your clinician if this reading keeps rising."**
    An instruction the reader may never open.
  </DoDont.Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

**Checked by `pnpm run check:a11y` on every commit.** Only neutral chrome roles,
no colour literal, a 44pt target in rem and no `px` type size.

**Argued from the source, not measured.**

- Base UI renders each header as an `h3` with a `button`, and wires
  `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` between trigger and panel.
- The chevron is `aria-hidden`. A reader relies on `aria-expanded`, not the glyph.
- The chevron turns over `--opsin-duration-fast`, which the product stylesheet
  collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Base UI unmounts a closed panel, so its content leaves the tab order and the
  accessibility tree.

**Not checked by any gate.** Every contrast pair against the card surface, and
whether the open section reads as open under `forced-colors: active`.

## API reference

<PropsTable name="AccordionProps" />

`multiple` defaults to `false`. `value` without `onValueChange` fixes the open
sections exactly as given. An empty `items` array renders nothing and raises a
development warning, and so does a duplicated `value`.
