## Accessibility

**Checked by `pnpm run check:a11y` on every commit.** Every colour is a
`--opsin-material-*` token or a bridged surface role, nothing resolves to
nothing under the product theme, and no `px` font size appears.

**Measured by `pnpm run contrast`.** Body text against each of the five content
rungs in both themes, in both APCA and WCAG 2.2, with `scrim` excluded.

**Argued from the source, not measured.**

- The surface is not announced. It has no role and no name, takes no focus, and
  its three decorative layers are empty and `aria-hidden`.
- No text is ever placed over an unscrimmed backdrop. The Backdrop renders only
  in the branch where the Scrim is unconditional.
- Reduced transparency and a missing `backdrop-filter` both reach a defined
  [opaque fallback](../accessibility/reduced-transparency.mdx), with the border,
  shadow and geometry left alone.
- `prefers-contrast: more` and `forced-colors: active` both drop the material,
  one [widening](../accessibility/increased-contrast.mdx) the edge and the other
  recolouring it to `CanvasText`.

**Not checked by any gate.** The composited translucent figures, the widened
edge against the non-text floor, a large-text scrim floor, the three-layer
budget, and the scrim at 200% text.

## API reference

<PropsTable name="SurfaceProps" />

`rung` is required, because no default depth is safe to guess. There is no
`blur`, `opacity`, `tint`, radius or `as` prop, and no `contentWeight`, because
no large-text scrim floor has been measured. A rung outside the six is refused.
Development warns and the children still render, with no material. `className`
is merged unrestricted, so the [two colour axes](../health/two-colour-axes.mdx)
rule applies in full.
