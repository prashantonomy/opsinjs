## What this name refers to

Progress towards a daily goal, drawn as a closing ring.

## Why it is not on the roster

Goal rings are strongly associated with one platform's fitness product, and the pattern rewards streaks — which is the wrong incentive for a reader managing a condition rather than training for one.

## What to use instead

Use [ScoreDial](score-dial.mdx) or [MetricTile](metric-tile.mdx) instead.
