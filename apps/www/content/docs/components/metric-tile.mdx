### States

| Data state | What MetricTile renders |
| --- | --- |
| Error | A `measuredAt` the tile cannot locate beside a reading is refused, and nothing renders. |
| Empty | `value={null}` keeps the label, drops the time line, and is not zero. |
| Stale | Past `staleAfterHours` the parts mute and the time line adds *may be out of date*. |

## Clinical meaning

**Asserts.** With a reading, that this measurement had this value, in this unit, at
this time, so `measuredAt` is required beside one. An absence is not a reading of zero.

**Never read as.** A current reading, unless it is one. Past the product's boundary the
tile mutes and says so in words, as
[Trends and uncertainty](../health/trends.mdx) requires.

**Colour axis.** Category, on the icon and the label only. Status appears as an embedded
[StatusPill](./status-pill.mdx), never the tile's background, so no element carries
both ([Two colour axes](../health/two-colour-axes.mdx)).

**Thresholds.** None. `staleAfterHours` has no default, because opsinjs holds no boundary
for any measurement, and omitting it means no stale treatment. The tile derives no level,
because `status` is the product's under [Two colour axes](../health/two-colour-axes.mdx).
Pair `attention` or `urgent` with an `href`, because nothing here enforces it.

**Provenance and masking.** There is no provenance prop and no mask, so do not route a
figure nobody measured through `measuredAt`.
[Trends and uncertainty](../health/trends.mdx) and
[Consent and privacy](../health/consent-and-privacy.mdx) name this component, and neither is met.

## Accessibility

The rendered pass found no axe violation, target-size hit, `focus-visible` failure or
reflow at 320px, and the source needed no fix.

**Residual items.**

- `Value` speaks *kilograms* for a visible *kg*, so a linked tile can trip WCAG 2.5.3
  Label in Name. The repair belongs in `Value` and is deferred, and the *steps* demo does
  not trip.
- Nothing enforces the JSDoc rule that `attention` and `urgent` pair with an `href`.
- Contrast as the tile composes it, `prefers-contrast: more`, `forced-colors`, 200% text,
  print and masking are unverified.

## Props interface

<PropsTable name="MetricTileProps" />

A `measuredAt` the tile cannot locate beside a reading renders nothing, and development
reports it once. `label`, `value`, `measuredAt`, `now` and `precision` are required with
no default. `value` is `number | null`, so a compound reading takes two
tiles. Read `now` once per screen and pass it to every tile.
