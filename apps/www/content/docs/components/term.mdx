## Clinical meaning

**Asserts.** That this word has this everyday meaning, according to the product's
glossary. That translation claim is the product's, not a clinician's. opsinjs ships an
empty glossary. See [Who this is for](../health/who-this-is-for.mdx).

**Never read as.** A statement about the reader's condition, or advice. *Chronic means
long-lasting* is a translation. *Chronic means you will have this for life* is a
prognosis, which the glossary is not for.

**Colour axis.** Neither. The mark is a dotted underline, which survives greyscale.
Tinting jargon by category makes a word look like a status, and tinting it by status
invents a clinical level nobody assigned. See
[Clinical interaction guidelines](../health/clinical-interaction-guidelines.mdx).

**Thresholds.** None. Term puts no reading on the screen, so it holds no threshold and
stamps no value.

**Gap you must build around.** A call site naming a `plain-only` entry gets the authored
sentence and a development warning, because a runtime cannot rewrite a sentence. The lint
rule that would catch it does not exist.

<SafetyCallout severity="attention" evidence="opinion">
Review the glossary as clinical content, not as microcopy. *Acute means sudden* is a
definition. *Acute means this will settle down on its own* is guidance with nobody
behind it. Nothing catches the drift.
</SafetyCallout>

## Examples

### Reading a paragraph without leaving it

Two terms is close to the ceiling for one paragraph. The third appearance carries `once`,
so the word stays marked and reachable but the definition is not printed again.

## Content guidelines

Definitions are one sentence, under about twenty words. Define the word, not the
condition, and never use a second piece of jargon inside one. Expansions are the full form
and nothing else. `auto` keeps a definition of about eighty characters or less beside the
word, so a tight sentence needs no press.

<DoDont>
  <DoDont.Do>
    **"eGFR (estimated glomerular filtration rate, an estimate of how well your kidneys
    are filtering)."** Expansion, then plain definition.
  </DoDont.Do>
  <DoDont.Dont>
    **"eGFR (estimated glomerular filtration rate)."** Every word in the expansion needs
    its own definition.
  </DoDont.Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

**Checked by `pnpm run check:a11y` on every pull request and every push to `main`.** No
type size is in `px` and no colour literal appears anywhere in the component.

**Argued from the source, not measured.**

- `present` has no `hover` value, so the definition never depends on hover.
- Collapsed, the definition is `display: none` but still reaches the reader as the
  trigger's description.
- The disclosure is a real button named by its visible word, as SC 2.5.3 Label in Name
  asks.
- The mark changes form, not colour: dotted at rest, solid while open. The definition is
  its next sibling.

**Not checked by any gate.** The 44 by 44 floor relies on the inline-target exception, and
two triggers on adjacent wrapped lines can overlap. No screen reader and no speech-input
software have been run. The interaction tints are unmeasured, and neither
`prefers-contrast` nor `forced-colors` is answered.

## API reference

<PropsTable name="TermProps" />

<PropsTable name="TermGlossaryProviderProps" />

There is no way to supply a definition at a call site. Definitions live in the glossary so
they stay consistent, reviewable and countable, and they arrive once through
`TermGlossaryProvider`. An `id` the glossary does not hold renders unmarked and warns in
development, printing `children` where a call site supplied any and otherwise the raw `id`.
