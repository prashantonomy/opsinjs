## Accessibility

The audit found nothing to change. The rendered pass was clean, with zero axe violations,
rows floored at `--opsin-target-minimum`, focus kept visible and no sideways scroll at
320px. The source has one roving tab stop, a visible legend wired by `aria-labelledby`
rather than an `aria-label`, and selection carried three ways through `aria-checked`, the
lifted ring and the filled dot, never on colour alone.

**Residual items.**

- An option's `description` renders inside the radio, so the accessible name folds it in
  rather than exposing it through `aria-describedby`. It is announced and passes AA, and
  wiring it as a distinct description restructures the name, a redesign rather than a fix.
- The colour transition on the row is not motion under 2.3.3, and its
  `prefers-reduced-motion` handling lives in global product CSS.
- Every colour is a role token, so no contrast ratio reads from the source. The measured
  pairs are the report below.

## Props interface

<PropsTable name="RadioGroupProps" />

`value` and `onValueChange` make this controlled, with no internal state. A `value`
matching no option renders nothing chosen and warns in development. A disabled
option stays in the list, drawn in the muted ink and skipped by the Arrow keys. No
options renders nothing.
