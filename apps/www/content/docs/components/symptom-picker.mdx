## What this name refers to

A picker for choosing symptoms from a controlled list.

## Why it is not on the roster

A symptom picker is one step from a symptom checker, and a symptom checker is a regulated device in most of the markets this system targets. The controlled vocabulary is also product-specific and cannot be shipped in a design system.

## What to use instead
