## What this name refers to

A record of medicine doses taken and missed.

## Why it is not on the roster

Dose data drives dosing decisions. A component that displays a missed dose is one product decision away from implying what to do about it, which is prescribing.

## What to use instead

Use [LogSheet](log-sheet.mdx) or [CareCard](care-card.mdx) instead.
