## Usage

```tsx
import { Popover } from "@/components/ui/popover"
```

```tsx
<Popover title="How readings are grouped" trigger="Why two lists?">
  <p>
    Entries you add yourself sit in one list, and entries from a connected
    device sit in another, so you can always tell which is which.
  </p>
</Popover>
```

## Content guidelines

Give every popover a `title`: the panel is a `role="dialog"`, and the title is
the name a screen reader announces. Name it for what it holds, keep the body to a
sentence or two, and never put a clinical reading or verdict loose inside one.

<DoDont>
  <DoDont.Do>
    **"How readings are grouped"** over one sentence: titled, short, easily dismissed.
  </DoDont.Do>
  <DoDont.Dont>
    **A blood pressure reading and its status** in an untitled panel: no name, no range, wrong surface.
  </DoDont.Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

**Checked by `pnpm run check:a11y` on every commit.**

- No status colour, no colour literal, no ramp step: only neutral chrome and the
  `overlay` shadow token.
- No `px` type size. The body is the `body` step, the title the `headline` step.
- The trigger's hit area is floored at `--opsin-target-minimum` in rem.

**Argued from the source, not measured.**

- The panel is a `role="dialog"` named by `aria-labelledby`. A missing title
  raises a development warning.
- Focus moves into the panel on open and back to the trigger on close, by Base UI.
- The arrow is `aria-hidden`.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the scale is pinned and only the opacity crossfades.

**Not checked by any gate.** Every contrast pair, the rendering under
`forced-colors: active`, and whether the arrow's bordered edges still face outward
after a collision flip or in right-to-left.

## API reference

<PropsTable name="PopoverProps" />

`trigger` is the button's visible content, not a control of your own; a button
passed here would nest inside another. `side` defaults to `bottom` and is a
preference the primitive overrides on a collision. `className` merges onto the
panel and must resolve neither colour axis.
