A blank tile, a dash and a "0" are three different assertions. "0 steps yesterday" is not the same fact as "no step data for yesterday".

## When to use it

Use it whenever a health surface renders with no value to show.

Do not use it when:

- The data exists but is old. That is [Offline and stale data](./offline-and-stale-data.mdx).
- The reader has never opened the product. That is [Onboarding and first run](./onboarding-and-first-run.mdx).
- A trend has too few points to support a direction. See [Trend review](./trend-review.mdx).

## How it works

A measured zero is a value, shown with its unit. Every other absence renders its reason:

| Kind of nothing | Render | Never imply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loading | A skeleton in the final layout's shape | A value |
| Never collected | One sentence of value and exactly one action | Nothing to worry about |
| Nothing in this filter | Say so, and offer to widen it | They stopped logging |
| Pending | What is expected, and by when | It is late or lost |
| Failed to load | An error with a retry | The value is zero or unchanged |
| Withheld | What is off, and how to turn it back on | The data was deleted |

- Never render an absent value as a number: no zero, no dash styled as data, no last-known value dressed as current.
- Never present a failed request as "no data yet". That is a fact about your network, not about the reader.
- Absence has no clinical status, so an empty tile carries no status colour. See [Two colour axes](../health/two-colour-axes.mdx).
- Loading has a floor and a ceiling. Never flash a skeleton, and after a stated timeout show an honest error.

## Components

- [EmptyState](../components/empty-state.mdx) requires a `reason`: `nothing-yet`, `no-matches`, `nothing-left`, `not-enough` or `could-not-load`. Only `could-not-load` draws a marker of its own, and its action is mandatory.
- [Skeleton](../components/skeleton.mdx) holds the layout while loading, and `appearAfterMs` stops it flashing on a fast load.
- [CareCard](../components/care-card.mdx) says when a pending result is expected.
- [MetricTile](../components/metric-tile.mdx) with `value={null}` keeps its label and never shows a zero.

## Content

- One fact, one action, one reason: "No blood-pressure readings yet. Log one and we'll show it against your range." "No data" says nothing.
- No jokes about missing health data.
- An error owns the problem: "We couldn't load your readings. This is a problem on our side. Try again."

## Accessibility

- The empty state has a heading at the level the content would have had, so the outline keeps its shape.
- Skeletons are `aria-hidden`, and the caller mounts one polite status message for the change. An error the reader might act on is announced assertively (WCAG 2.2 SC 4.1.3).
- The single action is the first focusable element after the heading.
- Illustrations are decorative, and nothing animates to fill the space.
