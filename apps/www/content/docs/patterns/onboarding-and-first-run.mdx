Every question asked before the first moment of value is asked on credit, and health questions are expensive. Some frighten, some are private, and some need a piece of paper to answer.

## When to use it

Use it between the reader first opening the app and first seeing something about them. A setup bar with more than four steps is a warning sign. Use something else for:

- a returning reader with an empty screen: [Empty and first use](./empty-and-first-use.mdx), never a re-run of setup;
- a device permission: [Consent and permissions](./consent-and-permissions.mdx);
- a validated questionnaire, even one that runs first: [Forms](./forms.mdx);
- how to ask for one detail, such as a date of birth: [Ask users for…](./ask-users-for.mdx).

## How it works

1. **Scope first.** One screen says what the product does, and that it is not a medical device or a substitute for advice. Late disclosure reads as concealment.
2. **Gate every field.** Answer in writing: can the first useful screen render without this? If it can, ask on the surface that needs it, where the reader sees why.
3. **Ask one question at a time, with a reason.** "Why we ask: your age changes the range we compare your readings to."
4. **Name the consequence of every skip.** "Skip. We will show ranges for adults generally rather than for your age." Skipping still arrives at a usable screen.
5. **Show the first value.** The reader's own number comes back with no status, no range verdict and no reassurance, because a first reading is not a baseline.
6. **Defer the rest.** Consent comes when a feature needs it, profile fields when a calculation needs them, and an account when something must be kept or synced.

Nothing that arrives later blocks the first value, and nothing is timed or advances by itself.

| State | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Resumed | Returns to the exact question with earlier answers intact |
| Skipped fields | Recorded as skipped and re-asked later in context, never on the next launch |
| Declined everything | An honest screen, never a locked one with no exit |

## Content

Spend the reader's attention on scope and the next step, not on features. "Understand your heart" is a description. "Improve your heart health" is a health claim, and a reader remembers a claim made at first run. No status colour appears, because first run has no clinical state to grade.

## Accessibility

- Each step has one heading, and focus moves to it on advance, never to the continue button.
- Progress is stated in text, such as "Step 2 of 4", not by dots alone.
- Back never destroys input (WCAG 2.2 SC 3.3.7), and no step has a time limit (SC 2.2.1).
- Sign-in needs no memory test (SC 3.3.8), so pasting a one-time code must work.
- Skip is a labelled, focusable control of at least 44pt that stays beside continue at 200% text.
