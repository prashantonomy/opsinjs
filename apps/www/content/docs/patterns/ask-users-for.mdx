The cheapest field is the one you do not collect, and the next cheapest is a coarser one, such as an age band rather than a birth date.

## When to use it

Use it before adding any field below. For how forms behave, see [Forms](./forms.mdx), and for when to ask, [Onboarding and first run](./onboarding-and-first-run.mdx). For wording, see [Writing](../foundations/writing.mdx).

Do not use it for a daily reading, which is [Daily logging](./daily-logging.mdx). Nothing here is legal advice.

## How it works

1. Name the decision the answer changes. If you cannot, do not ask.
2. Ask for the coarsest answer that decides it, and defer to the surface that needs it where you can.
3. Say why in one sentence beside the question. "A more personalised experience" is not a reason.
4. Say what skipping costs, and offer free text where a fixed list will not fit.
5. Never ask again for something already given (WCAG 2.2 SC 3.3.7). Let the reader edit it instead.

## Components

- [Field](../components/field.mdx) holds every typed answer, with its label, hint and error.
- [ReadingInput](../components/reading-input.mdx) takes height and weight with the unit beside the number.
- [RadioGroup](../components/radio-group.mdx) takes one answer from a short list, and [Checkbox](../components/checkbox.mdx) any number.
- [Combobox](../components/combobox.mdx) searches a list the product supplies.
- [Term](../components/term.mdx) explains any clinical or administrative word.

## Name

Ask for what the reader wants to be called, to address them. Ask for the name a clinic or insurer holds only to match a record, and say so.

- Use one free-text Field with `autocomplete="name"`. Split into `given-name` and `family-name` only when a receiving system demands parts, and never require a family name.
- Do not limit length or characters, change capitalisation, or trim more than the outer spaces.
- Address the reader by their preferred name everywhere. Use the record name only where a system requires it, and say so.
- An empty field gets "Enter your name", never "invalid name".

## Date of birth

Ask for an age band unless a decision needs the exact date, such as matching a clinical record. To check that someone is an adult, ask exactly that.

- Use three Fields, Day, Month and Year, in the reader's locale order, inside a `fieldset` whose `legend` is the question.
- Give each `inputmode="numeric"` and `autocomplete` of `bday-day`, `bday-month` or `bday-year`. Accept a month name, and require four-digit years.
- Never use a calendar picker as the only path, and never auto-advance between boxes.
- An error names the missing part and clears nothing. If your system accepts a year alone, say so.

## Sex and gender

These are two questions, or none. Ask sex only when a reference range, screening or calculation depends on it, and prefer the specific clinical question, such as whether the reader has periods. Ask gender or pronouns only to address the reader.

- Ask it with a RadioGroup labelled for its purpose: "Sex used for reference ranges".
- Never offer a closed list of two. Include intersex and "prefer not to say", and a route to a person.
- Take pronouns as free text with common options in a Field.
- Never derive one answer from the other, and keep both editable at any time.

## Ethnicity

Ask only for equity monitoring with an analysis plan, or for a clinical adjustment a named clinician owns. If you cannot name which, do not ask. Never ask on a results screen or in a symptom flow.

- Make it optional, self-identified and multi-select, with "prefer not to say" and free text. Use Checkboxes in a `fieldset`.
- Use a recognised national classification and name it. Never infer ethnicity from a name or a postcode.
- If it adjusts a number, the reader sees the adjustment and who owns it.

## Contact details

Ask for a channel only when you have a message to send on it. A message is a disclosure, because it may arrive on a lock screen somebody else can see.

- One channel per purpose, chosen by the reader. Use `type="email"` with `autocomplete="email"`, or `type="tel"` with `autocomplete="tel"`.
- Verify before relying on a channel, without locking the reader out. A code field takes `autocomplete="one-time-code"`, and paste works.
- The message says only that there is something to see, never the substance.
- Check an email for an `@` and let delivery test the rest. Store phone numbers as entered.

## Height and weight

Ask only when a named calculation needs it, and ask adult height once. Weight is a hard question for some readers, so never require it unless that calculation cannot run without it.

- Use ReadingInput with `units={["kg", "lb", "st"]}`, so switching converts what was typed.
- Compound units such as feet and inches take separate boxes under one legend.
- Bounds are physical, never typical. No praise, comparison or target the reader did not set.
- If you derive BMI, show its limits beside it, never show its category as a clinical status, and let the reader turn it off. If you cannot do all three, do not derive it.

## Medications

Ask only when the product does something specific with them. A self-entered list is what the reader believes they take, never a clinical record, and opsinjs does not support interaction checking against it.

- Search, do not spell. Use a Combobox over the product's own list, matching brand and generic names, because opsinjs ships no vocabulary.
- Keep a free-text Field always available. Never auto-select a result or autocorrect a name.
- Take strength as a number and a unit separately, then form and schedule in the reader's words.
- Show a read-back before storing, mark every entry self-reported or imported, and keep a stopped medicine with its dates.

## Symptoms

Record symptoms, never assess them, because assessment is a regulated clinical function opsinjs does not support. A symptom that needs action now, or any question that touches self-harm, belongs to [High-stakes moments](../health/high-stakes-moments.mdx).

- Put a [Textarea](../components/textarea.mdx) first and largest, inside a Field. [SymptomPicker](../components/symptom-picker.mdx), [BodyMap](../components/body-map.mdx) beside a checkbox list, and [ScaleInput](../components/scale-input.mdx) with written anchors are optional assists.
- Ask duration in relative words: "since this morning".
- A red-flag rule signposts help and never diagnoses, and the entry is saved either way.
- Show the entry back word for word, with no status colour.
