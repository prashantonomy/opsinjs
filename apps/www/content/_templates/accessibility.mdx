## What we guarantee

What opsinjs takes responsibility for, stated as a commitment a reviewer could
hold us to. Name the success criteria where they apply.

## What you own

What remains the implementing team's responsibility, and why it cannot be moved
into the system. Be specific enough to be actionable: "the accessible name of
your button" rather than "your content".

## How to check

Reproducible steps. A named tool or a keyboard sequence, an expected result, and
what a failure looks like. Somebody who has never used a screen reader should be
able to follow this and get a trustworthy answer.

the roadmap. A blank gaps section reads as a claim of completeness — if there are
genuinely none, say that in a sentence.
