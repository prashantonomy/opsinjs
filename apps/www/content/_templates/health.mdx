## What this does not cover

The boundary. Thresholds, reference ranges, triage and wording that carries
clinical meaning are things opsinjs does NOT decide. Say that plainly, and say
whose job each of them is. A health page that does not draw its own boundary
invites the reader to assume the system is doing more than it is.
