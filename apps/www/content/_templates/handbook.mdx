## The short version

The answer, first, in two or three sentences or one code block. A reader who
already knows this topic should be able to stop here.

## How it works

The mechanism underneath, in enough detail that the reader can debug it when it
goes wrong rather than only follow it when it goes right.

## Do this

The recommended approach, concretely.

## Not this

The approach people take instead, and what it costs. Name the failure, not just
the preference.

## Gotchas

The things that will actually waste an afternoon: version-specific behaviour,
silent no-ops, ordering that matters, errors whose message points at the wrong
file.

## Related

Two or three links with the reason to follow each.
